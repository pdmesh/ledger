Ledger
How it works Privacy User guide GitHub

Your statements, kept as one ledger.

Ledger reads the bank and credit card statements you already download, sorts every transaction into a category, and pairs up transfers and card payments so nothing is counted twice. It runs on your Mac, and your data stays there.

Download for Mac Free and open source under the MIT license. For Macs with Apple silicon.

What happens when you add a statement

Drop in PDFs, CSVs or spreadsheets, or point Ledger at the folder your downloads go to. You don't review every row; you check the few things it isn't sure about.

  1. It works out what the file is

    Bank, account, whose account it is, and the statement period. A new account is created the first time it's seen.

  2. It reads the transactions

    From the statement's own table where it can, and with an AI model when the layout is unusual or the PDF is a scan. Password-protected PDFs wait until you unlock them.

  3. It tidies and categorises

    "DOORDASH*CHIPOTLE SAN FRANCISCO CA" becomes DoorDash, in Food delivery. Your rules and past corrections come first; your corrections are never overwritten.

  4. It pairs up the same money

    Transfers between your accounts, card bill payments, refunds and duplicate rows from overlapping statements are linked, so every dollar is counted once.

  5. You check what's uncertain

    Low-confidence categories and new matches are one filter away. Totals per statement let you compare against the bill in seconds.

Your finances stay on your Mac

Ledger isn't a service. There's no account to create, no server holding your data, and it doesn't send analytics or check for updates.

If you choose to use a cloud AI service, it receives transaction descriptions and amounts, and whole statements when it has to read one. Use a local model instead and nothing leaves your computer.

  • One folder, on your diskThe ledger database and your original statements, stored unchanged.
  • Keys and passwords in the keychainAPI keys and PDF passwords are kept by macOS, never in the database.
  • Only the last four digitsFull account and card numbers aren't stored.
  • Undo an importDelete a statement and every transaction from it goes too.
  • Backups you controlOne zip with everything, saved where you choose.

Works without AI. Better with the one you already use.

Rules, merchant mappings and your corrections categorise transactions on their own. Add a model for the rest, and Ledger picks it per task, with fallbacks if one fails.

A local model

Ollama, LM Studio or any OpenAI-compatible server on your machine. Nothing leaves your computer.

Claude Code or Codex

Use the CLI you're already signed in to. Ledger runs it in a sandboxed temporary folder and never sees your login.

Your own API key

Anthropic, OpenAI, Gemini and others. Every call is listed with its cost; request contents are never logged.

Get Ledger

Download the app for Macs with Apple silicon. It isn't signed by Apple yet, so the first time you open it, choose Open Anyway in System Settings → Privacy & Security.
